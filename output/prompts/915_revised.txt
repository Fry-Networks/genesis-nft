Creating an anthropomorphic figure from an unconventional food, our focus is a cyberpunk humanoid french fry character. The character's body showcases the golden-yellow hues characteristic of a perfectly cooked, thick-cut fry. It stands confidently against a landscape of a rain-soaked, neon-lit rooftop, the puddle reflections further intensifying the overall mood. The character exudes charisma with small, friendly oval eyes nestled comfortably on its potato exterior. Attired in a utility vest swathed with pouches and cables, it dons a sleek open-face cyber helmet crowned with a lifelike HUD visor. In its grip is a data tablet, its screen alive with fluctuating charts. The skin of this captivating character is traced with a network of blue circuit line patterns, each glowing subtly. We capture this portrait in a square composition, focusing on the bust from the shoulders up. The choice of lighting is dark and moody, throwing into relief the high contrast, cinematic composition, and the meticulously detailed textures. Our french fry character dominates the center of the frame, appearing cool, confident, and slightly sinister. The crispness of the potato's exterior is painstakingly rendered, devoid of any text, watermarks, or logos. The overarching goal? To create a work of art with a deep-rooted cyberpunk vibe.
